## 🎯 Model Specifications

### Architecture
- **Type:** Control Panel Architecture
- **Input Sheet:** Assumptions (single source of truth)
- **Calculation Sheets:** 9 worksheets auto-calculate from Assumptions
- **Color Coding:** Blue = inputs, Yellow = calculations
- **Formula Structure:** All formulas link back to Assumptions sheet

### Functionality
- **Projection Period:** 10 years (configurable)
- **Timeline:** Monthly granularity (120 months)
- **CAPEX Phasing:** 5 phases with custom construction periods
- **Revenue Streams:** 5 streams (seed, ware potato, fries, fresh-pack, feed)
- **Cost Categories:** 5 categories (seed, outgrower, processing, overhead, logistics)
- **Debt Types:** Senior debt + subordinated debt + grants
- **Scenarios:** Base, Downside, Upside (one-click toggle)
- **Returns Metrics:** IRR, NPV, DSCR, payback, margins, coverage ratios

### Technical Details
- **Built with:** Python 3.11 + openpyxl library
- **Compatible with:** Excel 2016+, Google Sheets
- **File Format:** .xlsx (OpenXML)
- **Formulas:** Native Excel formulas (no macros)
- **No External Links:** Fully self-contained
